Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Split Wise ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile and web application (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo as provided by Google. We do not store your Google password.
  • Trip Data: Trip names, member names, expense records, split details, currency amounts, settlement records, and WhatsApp group links you choose to enter.
  • Device Identity: A unique, anonymous device identifier used to associate you with your trips. This is a randomly generated UUID and is not linked to your personal identity beyond your own device.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: App interaction patterns (screens viewed, features used) collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.
  • Crash Reports: Crash and performance data collected via Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix bugs.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for compatibility and support purposes.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Financial account numbers, credit/debit card details, or banking credentials
  • Biometric data
  • Precise geolocation (GPS coordinates)
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Camera, microphone, or media library access (unless explicitly requested for photo attachments)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core functionality (expense tracking, splitting, settlement calculations)
  • To synchronize your trip data across your devices via Firebase Realtime Database
  • To enable sharing of trip invite codes and expense summaries
  • To communicate with you regarding support requests sent to our support email
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security vulnerabilities
  • To improve the App through aggregated, anonymized usage analytics

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: Your trip and expense data is primarily stored locally on your device using SQLite (via the Drift database library). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Cloud Sync.
  • Cloud Sync (Optional): If enabled, trip data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and stored in Firebase Realtime Database. Cloud Sync data is associated with your device identifier, not your personal identity.
  • Encryption: All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS encryption.
  • Access Controls: Firebase Security Rules ensure that your trip data is accessible only by members of that specific trip.
  • Data Retention: We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may delete your data at any time through the App or by contacting us.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Trip Members: Trip data (expenses, settlements, member names) is shared with other members of the same trip as necessary for the App's functionality.
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google LLC) for hosting, database, analytics, and authentication services. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may only process it in accordance with our instructions.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred with prior notice.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Hosting, Realtime Database, Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: For account authentication only. We receive only your name, email, and profile photo. Google Privacy Policy
  • Exchange Rate APIs: For currency conversion. Only the currency pair and amount are transmitted; no personal information is shared.
  • WhatsApp: For sharing trip links. We do not access WhatsApp data; we only open a pre-filled message link that you choose to send.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Portability: You may export your trip data at any time through the App's settings.
  • Correction: You may edit your profile information and trip data at any time within the App.
  • Deletion: You may sign out and delete local data by clearing the App's storage. To request deletion of cloud-synced data, contact us at the email below.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: You may disable Firebase Analytics through your device settings.
  • Cloud Sync: You may disable Cloud Sync at any time. Disabling does not delete previously synced data; contact us to request deletion.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Firebase servers are operated by Google LLC in various global regions. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers, including standard contractual clauses where required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
